What is Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Lending?

Peer-to-Peer lending is an alternative finance model where individual lenders directly fund credit-worthy borrowers through a secure technology portal. By removing the traditional banking intermediary, borrowers access fair rates and lenders earn direct interest yields.

🏦

Traditional Bank Model

Intermediary retains the spread

You deposit savings at 3.5%–6.5% interest. The bank lends those funds to borrowers at 12%–20%, capturing the remaining margin to cover corporate overhead and branch operations.

  • ✗ Low interest on savings (3.5%) & FDs (~6.5%)
  • ✗ Opaque: Depositors never know how capital is utilized
  • ✗ Rigid paperwork and approval delays for personal credit
  • ✗ Prepayment penalties and high administrative fees
🌐

LendCircle Direct Model

Direct, Transparent & Fractional

Borrowers post loan requests with custom tenures and interest rates. Lenders review transparent credit metrics and fund loans in small fractions, earning monthly interest directly.

  • ✓ Direct Returns: 100% of interest paid by borrowers goes to lenders
  • ✓ Fractional Diversification: Spread ₹10,000 across 10 to 20 loans
  • ✓ Transparent Profiles: Bureau credit scores & verified income
  • ✓ Zero Prepayment Penalties: Borrowers can prepay anytime
User Journeys

Clear Steps for Borrowers & Lenders

Whether you need financing or want to deploy capital, LendCircle provides structured, easy-to-follow steps.

1

Digital KYC Verification

Register as a Borrower. Submit your Aadhaar or PAN card. Administrators verify identity to ensure a secure platform for all participants.

2

Post Loan Request

Enter required principal (₹1,000–₹20,00,000), tenure (3–60 months), and offered rate. The system generates an instant amortization preview.

3

Peer Funding & Disbursal

Individual lenders fund your request in full or partial amounts. Upon 100% funding, the loan is auto-created and disbursed to your wallet.

4

Repay & Build Credit Score

Pay monthly EMIs from your digital wallet. Each on-time payment awards +10 credit score points, unlocking better terms for future loans.

1

Lender Identity Check

Register as a Lender and complete PAN verification. Define your portfolio limit to manage your exposure responsibly.

2

Deposit Into Digital Wallet

Add capital to your LendCircle wallet. Wallets are protected by non-negative balance database constraints (`balance >= 0.00`).

3

Browse & Fractionalize

Filter open requests by interest rate, tenure, and borrower credit score. Spread funds in small increments across multiple borrowers to mitigate risk.

4

Receive Monthly EMIs

When borrowers pay EMIs, our stored procedure automatically distributes principal and interest proportionally into your wallet. Withdraw or reinvest anytime.

⚠️ Risk Disclosure & Non-Guaranteed Disclaimer

Peer-to-Peer lending is an unsecured credit model. Loan returns are dependent on borrower repayment behavior and are not guaranteed. LendCircle does not provide government insurance (such as DICGC) or bank guarantees. Lenders must conduct prudent risk evaluation and diversify funds across multiple loans. Fractional lending allows you to fund loans in micro-tickets (from ₹500 upwards). For example, allocating ₹10,000 across 10 borrowers ensures that even if one borrower encounters repayment difficulty, 90% of your capital remains actively amortizing, preserving your portfolio health.
