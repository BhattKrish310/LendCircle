Risk Management

Fractional Lending: The Golden Rule of Risk Mitigation

In unsecured peer lending, default risk is minimized through diversification. Rather than lending ₹50,000 to one borrower, LendCircle lets you lend ₹1,000 to 50 different verified borrowers.

  • ✓ Single-Borrower Cap: No single borrower holds more than a small fraction of your portfolio.
  • ✓ Smoother Returns: Occasional delays from one borrower are offset by the remaining 49 on-time payments.
  • ✓ Compounding Yields: Reinvest monthly EMI credits back into fresh loans.

P2P lending does not provide guaranteed returns. Loan performance depends entirely on borrower repayment capacity and economic conditions. LendCircle enforces credit scoring, risk categorization, and overdue supervision, but cannot guarantee recovery of all principal. Never lend money that you cannot afford to have delayed or at risk.
